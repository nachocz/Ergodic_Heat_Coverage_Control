# Background music

The optional background music of the web pages (toggle ♪ in the top bar) plays
seven calm piano pieces: three by Erik Satie (1866-1925) in public-domain
recordings, and four by Kevin MacLeod under the Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 license. All of them can be redistributed with this project; the four
CC BY pieces require the attribution given below.

## Changes

All files were re-encoded to 96 kbit/s stereo MP3 with loudness normalization
(about -22 LUFS) and short fades, so that the pieces play at a similar, quiet
level and work in every browser. The Satie recordings were converted from the
original Ogg Vorbis files; the Kevin MacLeod pieces from the original MP3
files, otherwise unchanged.
